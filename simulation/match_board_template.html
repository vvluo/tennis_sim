Match Simulations

Point-by-point output from the tennis simulator. Every point carries the player who won it, the number of shots it lasted, and the running game score. Open a match for its statistics, sets, games and points. Aces are not listed: the simulator records an unreturned serve as a one-shot point and cannot tell a clean ace from a serve that was reached but not returned.
