Four attributes per player, each scaled 0–10 within its own tour, from 18,891 player-match rows across 9,690 tour-level matches, 25 Aug 2025 – 25 Aug 2026. Ratings are credited for the level of opposition faced and pulled toward the low end where the evidence is thin.

Ratings are relative within each tour, never across — WTA return rates run about 0.85σ above ATP, so a shared scale would read that gap as skill. The 40–100 view maps each rating with floor(40 + 6 × r), truncated so a figure never reads higher than it is, and tiers cut the overall at 90 / 80 / 65. Ratings resting on thin evidence are shrunk toward the low end by x = max(2p−1, 1−matches/10), where p is the share of that rating’s own inputs a player is missing — so a low figure on a short match count reflects how little is known, not a measured weakness.
