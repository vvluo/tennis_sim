About this site

Sportradar box scores become per-player rates — serves in, points won on each serve, returns, winners, unforced errors, and the same figures from the other side of the net — averaged over a rolling twelve months and z‑scored against the tour.

Each z‑score is folded into SRV, RET, SHOT or CONS — oriented by how a unit of that rating moves that statistic in the simulator’s own point model, and weighted by how repeatably the statistic measures the same player — then scaled 0–10 and averaged into OVR, the ratings shown on the Tour Ratings Board.

Every match on this site is simulated from those ratings. Not affiliated with the ATP, WTA or any tournament.
